### About Us

Morning Star Empowerment Foundation is a Kenya-based Public Benefit Organization (PBO) headquartered in Mosoriot Town, Nandi County. Its purpose is to reduce poverty and improve livelihoods by empowering people to become self-reliant, healthy, skilled, economically productive, and environmentally responsible.

**Vision:** To build empowered, healthy, educated, economically independent, and environmentally responsible communities free from extreme poverty.

**Mission:** To elevate people from poverty through modern agriculture, youth empowerment, rehabilitation and counseling, vocational education, environmental conservation, and sustainable economic development.

### Our Programs

- **Modern agriculture and agribusiness development** — The Foundation aims to improve agricultural productivity and profitability by promoting modern farming technologies and practices from planting to marketing.
- **Alcohol and substance abuse mitigation and youth empowerment** — The Foundation seeks to reduce alcohol and substance abuse among young people and other vulnerable members of society through prevention, rehabilitation, counseling, mentorship, and community support.
- **Education, skills development, and vocational training** — The Foundation aims to empower young people and other community members with practical skills that improve employability, entrepreneurship, and self-reliance.
- **Environmental conservation and climate change mitigation** — The Foundation seeks to protect the environment, promote sustainable resource use, and help communities respond to the challenges of climate change.
- **Economic empowerment, savings, credit, and investment** — The Foundation aims to promote financial inclusion and economic independence by encouraging collective savings, responsible lending, entrepreneurship, and investment.

### How We Work

The Foundation’s implementation approach includes:

- Community mobilization and participation.
- Training, mentorship, and capacity building.
- Partnerships with government institutions, development agencies, and other stakeholders.
- Resource mobilization, fundraising, and responsible financial management.
- Establishment of community projects and demonstration sites.
- Monitoring, evaluation, and reporting on programme results.
- Promotion of inclusion, transparency, accountability, and sustainability.

### Page introduction

**About Morning Star Empowerment Foundation**

Morning Star Empowerment Foundation is a Kenya-based organization headquartered in Mosoriot Town, Nandi County. Its purpose is to reduce poverty and improve livelihoods by helping individuals, families, and communities build skills, self-reliance, economic opportunity, health, and environmental responsibility.

### Vision

To build empowered, healthy, educated, economically independent, and environmentally responsible communities free from extreme poverty.

### Mission

To elevate people from poverty through modern agriculture, youth empowerment, rehabilitation and counseling, vocational education, environmental conservation, and sustainable economic development.

### Our objective

We aim to promote sustainable livelihoods, improve access to skills and opportunities, support vulnerable groups, and encourage economic independence and community development.

### Who we work to serve

Our intended beneficiaries include young people and young adults; farmers and agribusiness entrepreneurs; people and families affected by poverty or substance use; school leavers; women and vulnerable households; small-scale traders; community groups; and communities affected by environmental degradation and climate change.

### Our approach

We bring communities and partners together around practical training, mentorship, capacity building, community projects, and demonstration activities. We aim to work with government institutions, development partners, community organizations, churches, professionals, and other stakeholders. Monitoring, evaluation, inclusion, transparency, accountability, and sustainability guide our approach.
